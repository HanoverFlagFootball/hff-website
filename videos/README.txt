HFF HOMEPAGE HERO VIDEO

Put your homepage highlight video in this folder and name it:

    hff-hero.mp4

The homepage is already configured to autoplay it muted, loop continuously, and play inline on phones.
If the video is missing or cannot load, TiCats.png remains visible as the fallback image.

For best website performance, use a short 10-20 second MP4, preferably 1080p or 720p and compressed for web.
