Hanover Flag Football

About HFF

Hanover Flag Football was established in 2025 to provide youth in Hanover and the surrounding area with an opportunity to play organized flag football. Our focus is simple: fun, competitive, affordable, and well organized.

For our first two seasons, HFF has operated U6, U9, U12, and U16 divisions. Divisions are reviewed each season and may change based on registration numbers so we can create the best possible experience and competition for our players.

Our Values

Teamwork  •  Dedication  •  Sportsmanship  •  Fun

We want players to develop their football skills while learning to work as a team, give their best effort, and show respect for teammates, opponents, coaches, and officials.

House League

The HFF House League is a nine-week season that combines development with organized competition. Games are played locally in Hanover and are officiated by local student referees.

Players of all skill and experience levels are welcome. The emphasis stays on teamwork, dedication, sportsmanship, development, and fun.
Week 1

Exhibition Games

Players compete with their new teams. Trades may be made afterward to help create fair and competitive teams.

Week 2

Team Practices

A dedicated practice week gives teams time to build chemistry and prepare for the regular season.

Weeks 3–8

Regular Season

Six weeks of scheduled, refereed games where standings and playoff positioning take shape.

Week 9

Super Bowl Weekend

The season finishes with playoffs and championship games.

Rep Program

Hanover Hustle

Hanover Hustle is where HFF's top athletes take their game beyond the house league.

Built for players ready to compete at a higher level, Hustle teams represent Hanover on the tournament stage against elite competition from across Ontario and beyond.

In 2026, Hanover Hustle made its mark at the Under Armour Flag Football World Cup in Brampton, Ontario — competing on one of the biggest stages in youth flag football.

Wearing the Hustle jersey means more than playing rep football. It means earning the opportunity to compete, develop, and represent Hanover against some of the best.
